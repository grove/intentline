## Delivered behavior

- `slice preview/record/status/render`: canonical source-bound advisory records,
  explicit same-parent supersession, 256-record immutable history, idempotent retry,
  secure cooperative locking and pointer-last publication with observed-race recovery.
  NO SPLIT has no children and preserves direct parent delivery.
- SPLIT validates 2–8 independently described children, S#:C# references, complete
  R allocation in both directions, original boundaries/source citations, conservative
  inherited constraints/exclusions, planned evidence or explicit gaps, observable
  prerequisite DAG and named full-parent integrated verification owners. Full roles
  require complete original statement/oracle/support; structural checks do not infer
  semantic completeness. Enabling steps never receive full product outcome credit.
- Packaged `intentline-slice` supports one ordinary request through the existing
  command-capable agent, with internal JSON/hash handling and no mandatory extra
  model/audit. The skill compares direct delivery and credible merges; only concrete
  observable, compatibility, recovery, prerequisite or ownership benefits justify split.
- `slice audit-record` captures optional actor-unverified exact-plan reports under
  a pointer-last index. Disagreements persist; favorable reports cannot erase adverse
  reports. No identity, reviewer independence, adoption or proof is authenticated.
- `slice impact` reads validated same-source revisions and transitive archived
  successors, distinguishes changed/added/retired and unchanged candidates, exposes
  recorded mappings, and conservatively marks allocations/dependencies/checks for
  review. Matching IDs, preserved labels or reversion do not transfer authority.
- `slice export/inspect` preserves whole parent/source/continuity/allocation context,
  inherited obligations and integration owners in bounded private advisory packages.
  An offline stdlib helper works under isolated Python without importing Intentline;
  installed inspection executes no bundle code. Rehashed omissions, corruption,
  missing ancestors and aborted output refuse. Private audit/adoption payloads are
  excluded; child acceptance and authorization remain not_established.

The existing native/direct Proofline routes stay standalone-only and reject child
packages. No child adapter, external repository change, model dispatch, issue
hierarchy, tracker effect, recursive decomposition, delivery runner or Lore cache
was added. Production remains Python 3.11+, stdlib, offline and model-free.
