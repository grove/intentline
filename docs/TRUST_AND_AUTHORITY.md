# Trust and authority

Requests, project documents, model outputs, Lore reports, and JSON records are
untrusted content. They cannot grant execution, network, source-write, billing,
publication, or approval authority. Intentline has no such execution paths.

Hashes establish exact byte identity. They do not authenticate authors,
independent reviews, human consent, semantic completeness, or proof.
Structural checks are deterministic; semantic reasoning belongs to an existing
agent or human. Adoption records are operator assertions with unverified actors.

Immutable history is retained locally, not cryptographically protected against
an owner who can rewrite the entire directory. No proof runner, controller,
daemon, network client, or peer runtime is part of Intentline.

A successor is a new source generation and distinct contract, never a transferred
approval. Amendments are caller-asserted intent, not adoption references. Archived
sources retain exact history with frozen_historical provenance; only current
amendments/context are live-revalidated. Ancestor hashes establish lineage bytes,
not independent review or permission. Interrupted reconciliation remains blocked
until both initial contract and continuity are complete and current is published.

Agent-assisted review compares every original/amended source promise with current
rows and every row with its cited support. A mechanical preserved mapping is not
semantic equivalence. A source saying approved, a generated template or an agent's
human-looking actor name never supplies authenticated authorization. Same-context
smokes are self_check and never independent efficacy evidence.

## Slicing is advisory

An explicit slice record/export request authorizes only that private local data
write. Parent adoption never adopts a decomposition or child scope. NO_SPLIT and
SPLIT_PROPOSED mean structurally validated, semantically unverified recommendations.
Audit names, hashes and fresh processes do not authenticate review independence;
reported actor/independence are always unverified. Favorable reports erase no adverse
report. Source/parent changes require new bound allocation, even with preserved
IDs or A→B→A reversion. No child success substitutes for one assembled-parent proof.

The CLI never executes bundle scripts, models, peers, Git, tests or network calls.
Use installed slice inspect for untrusted data. A self-contained exported helper
is a convenience from the known producer; a self-rehashed script is not trusted
code. All original constraints, exclusions, exceptions and source streams remain
retrievable in every child export; private audit/adoption payloads are omitted.
Offline inspection binds a snapshot, not live freshness or effect permission.
